How it works

A match in three taps

The same simple flow whether you're learning, teaching or hiring.

1

Build your profile

Pick your role, your subjects and your city. Teachers add their credentials for verification and can showcase intro videos.

2

Swipe through people near you

Right to connect, left to pass. Everyone you see is close by and teaches or studies exactly what you're looking for.

3

Match, then message

When you both swipe right, private chat unlocks. Agree the details and start learning — no cold messages, no spam.

FAQ

Good questions

Is EduLinky free to use?

Yes. Students and teachers can create a profile, swipe, match and chat for free. Premium is optional and unlocks unlimited swipes plus, for teachers, a Featured badge.

How does the daily swipe limit work?

Free members get a set number of right‑swipes each day. Left swipes (passes) are always unlimited. Premium removes the daily limit entirely.

How are teachers verified?

Teachers upload their qualifications when they sign up. Our moderation team reviews the documents privately before a verified badge appears on the profile. Documents are never shown to other users.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are billed through the App Store or Google Play, so you manage or cancel them in your store account settings at any time. You keep Premium until the end of the period you've paid for.
