# EduLinky — Beta Tester Guide 🎓

Thank you for helping test **EduLinky**! You're one of the first people to try it,
and your feedback directly shapes the app before launch. This guide walks you
through installing it, what to try, and how to report anything that looks wrong.

**It's a test build — please explore freely.** Tap everything, try to break things,
and don't worry about "messing up." That's exactly what we need.

---

## 📱 What is EduLinky?

EduLinky connects **students**, **teachers**, and **schools/institutions** in a
simple, swipe-based way — think of it as an easy way to find the right tutor,
student, or teaching opportunity.

You can sign up as any of these roles:
- **Student** — swipe through teachers, match, and chat.
- **Teacher** — get discovered by students, and browse teaching jobs.
- **Institution / School** — post jobs and connect with teachers.

Feel free to create more than one account (see "Test accounts" below) so you can
experience it from different sides.

---

## 1. Installing via TestFlight

TestFlight is Apple's official app for trying pre-release apps.

1. **Install TestFlight** from the App Store (search "TestFlight", by Apple).
2. **Accept your invite** — you'll get one of:
   - an **email invitation** → tap **View in TestFlight**, or
   - a **public link** → open it on your iPhone and tap **Accept**.
3. In TestFlight, tap **Install** next to EduLinky.
4. Open the app and sign up. Done!

> When a new test version is ready, TestFlight will show an **Update** button —
> please keep it up to date so you're testing the latest fixes.

**Requirements:** an iPhone running **iOS 15 or newer**.

## 2. Signing in

You can create an account with:
- **Email + password**
- **Continue with Apple**
- **Continue with Google**

All three are fine to use. If you sign up with Apple or Google, you'll still be
asked to pick your role and city — that's expected.

### Test accounts
- Use a **real email** you can access (some features send email).
- It's totally fine to make **several accounts** with different roles so you can,
  for example, match a student with a teacher and chat between them.
- 💡 Tip: to test matching and chat, sign in with one account on your phone and
  ask another tester (or use a second device) for the other side.

## 3. What to try

Please poke around everywhere, but here are the key things we'd love feedback on:

### As a Student
- [ ] Complete your profile (photo, city, subject).
- [ ] Swipe through teachers in **Discover** (pass / connect).
- [ ] Get a **match** and start a **chat**.
- [ ] Open a teacher's **profile** and leave a **review / rating**.
- [ ] Try **blocking** and **reporting** a user (safety features).

### As a Teacher
- [ ] Fill out your profile — subjects, experience, qualifications, videos, gallery.
- [ ] Upload a certificate for verification.
- [ ] Get discovered / matched by students and chat.
- [ ] Browse the **Jobs** tab and apply to a job.

### As an Institution / School
- [ ] Set up your institution profile.
- [ ] Post a **job** (with subject, location, details).
- [ ] Review teachers who apply.

### Everyone
- [ ] **Notifications** — do you get notified about matches, messages, etc.?
- [ ] Editing your profile and settings.
- [ ] Signing out and back in.
- [ ] General feel — is anything confusing, slow, or ugly?

## 4. Please note (test build quirks)

- 💳 **Payments/subscriptions are in testing.** You will **not** be charged real
  money. If prompted to pay, it's a sandbox test — do not enter real card details.
- 🔔 **Notifications** only work on a real iPhone (not needed to worry about — you're
  on one!). Make sure you tap **Allow** when the app asks.
- 🌐 Some content is still placeholder and wording may change.
- ⏳ TestFlight builds **expire after 90 days** — you may occasionally need to
  reinstall or update.
- 🧪 This is test data — please don't share anything private or sensitive in
  profiles or chats.

## 5. How to report a bug or give feedback 🐞

**The easiest way — right inside TestFlight:**
1. Take a **screenshot** in the app (side button + volume up).
2. TestFlight will pop up **"Share Beta Feedback"** — tap it, scribble/annotate,
   add a note, and **Send**. It reaches me automatically with your device details.

You can also **open TestFlight → EduLinky → Send Beta Feedback** any time.

**Or contact me directly:**
- 📧 Email: **[YOUR EMAIL HERE]**
- 💬 [Zalo / WhatsApp / group chat — YOUR CHOICE HERE]

### What makes a great bug report
The more of this you can include, the faster I can fix it:

| Include | Example |
|---|---|
| **What you did** | "Signed up as a teacher, tapped Jobs" |
| **What happened** | "The screen went white and nothing loaded" |
| **What you expected** | "I expected to see a list of jobs" |
| **Screenshot / screen recording** | (attach if you can) |
| **Your role** | Student / Teacher / Institution |
| **Your iPhone + iOS version** | e.g. iPhone 13, iOS 17.5 |

Even a quick "this button is confusing" is genuinely helpful — no report is too small.
